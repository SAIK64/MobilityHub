## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for academic mobility management, international relations offices, exchange coordinators, and traveling scholars. The aesthetic combines institutional authority with organic warmth, moving deliberately away from sterile, utilitarian enterprise software toward an elevated botanical and editorial atmosphere.

### Aesthetic Pillars
- **Curated Scholarly Poise**: Evokes archival materials, botanical research libraries, and historical campuses, translated into a modern digital platform.
- **Organic Precision**: Structured grids, high legibility, and architectural proportions tempered by a palette derived from deep moss, forest canopies, and pale mist.
- **Calm Operational Clarity**: Interfaces remain unhurried. Density is managed through deliberate tonal zoning, generous negative space, and typographic cadence rather than visual clutter.

## Colors

The palette balances authoritative deep forest charcoals with verdant foliage and misted surfaces.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#334A26`)**: Deep forest moss. Reserved for primary brand touchpoints, dominant navigational containers, high-order headers, and active state anchors.
- **Secondary / Accent (`#6D9159`)**: Vibrant moss sage. Drives direct interaction, primary call-to-action buttons, active switches, status indicators, and contextual badges.
- **Tertiary / Container Surface (`#E9F1E4`)**: Pale mist sage. Acts as secondary layer grounding, table header backgrounds, subtle structural borders, and neutral tag fills.
- **Neutral Foreground (`#11160E`)**: Deep forest charcoal. Used for primary typography, authoritative headlines, structural dividers, and iconography. Replaces pure black with an organic, ink-like depth.
- **Base Canvas & Cards (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure crisp white. Used for foreground interactive cards, data tables, and input fields to maintain high contrast and optical clarity against `#E9F1E4` backgrounds.

### Functional Roles
- **Canvas Base**: `#F4F8F1` (Subtle off-white pale moss) to `#E9F1E4` for main application canvases.
- **Elevated Canvas**: `#FFFFFF` for primary work surfaces and focal cards.
- **State Feedback**: Warning states skew toward amber-lichen tones, error states lean into deep oxidized madder, and success states draw from bright foliage greens.

## Typography

The typography leverages Plus Jakarta Sans across all display, editorial, and transactional roles. Its geometric foundation combined with rounded, humanized terminals mirrors the architectural and organic synthesis of the design system.

### Hierarchy & Composition Rules
- **Headlines (`headline-xl`, `headline-lg`, `headline-md`)**: Rendered in `#11160E` with negative letter-spacing for tight, authoritative phrasing. Use `headline-xl` primarily for institutional dashboards and overarching program titles.
- **Body (`body-lg`, `body-md`, `body-sm`)**: Set with generous line heights to facilitate prolonged review of mobility applications, bilateral agreements, and transcripts. Default to `body-md` for standard tabular data and narrative descriptions.
- **Labels & Microcopy (`label-lg`, `label-md`, `label-sm`)**: Set in semi-bold and medium weights with slight positive tracking to preserve legibility in uppercase administrative pills, progress steps, and data badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt baseline rhythm and a 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to display multi-pane administrative views, applicant dossiers, and institutional matrices without vertical exhaustion.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column grid, `margin`: `2.5rem`, `gutter`: `1.5rem`. Max content wrapper capped at 1440px for editorial alignment, expanding fluidly for complex data-dense grids.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column grid, `margin-tablet`: `1.5rem`, `gutter`: `1.25rem`. Multi-pane inspectors collapse into progressive tabs or split sheets.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px)**: 4-column grid, `margin-mobile`: `1rem`, `gutter-mobile`: `1rem`. Sidebars collapse into an off-canvas drawer; tables transition into card-based summary cards.

### Spacing Tokens Application
- `space-xs` (4px): Micro-spacing between paired icons and text labels.
- `space-sm` (8px): Internal tag padding, tight stacked metadata, list row vertical gaps.
- `space-md` (16px): Standard form element gap, button padding, inner card margins.
- `space-lg` (24px): Card interior boundaries, data block separation, table section breaks.
- `space-xl` (40px): Section breaks across administrative workflows.
- `space-2xl` (64px): Hero block paddings and primary canvas transitions.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is achieved through tonal stacking and organic ambient diffusion rather than artificial hard drop shadows. Depth mimics layered archival paper resting on a shaded forest desk.

### Surface Tiers & Stacking
1. **Canvas Tier (Level 0)**: `#F5F9F2` or `#E9F1E4`. Ground level for main application canvases. No elevation.
2. **Structural Panel Tier (Level 1)**: `#FFFFFF`. Elevated above canvas using a 1px border (`#D8E3D2`) and an ultra-subtle ambient shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(17, 22, 14, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(17, 22, 14, 0.02)`.
3. **Interactive & Floating Panels (Level 2)**: Modals, flyout menus, and active drag states sit on `#FFFFFF` with an expanded botanical tint shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(17, 22, 14, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(51, 74, 38, 0.06)`.
4. **Focused Overlays (Level 3)**: Institutional dialogs and review drawers with backdrop blur (`blur(6px)`) and a muted veil in `rgba(17, 22, 14, 0.4)`.

### Border Rationale
Borders are delicate, low-contrast membranes (`#D8E3D2` or `rgba(51, 74, 38, 0.12)`) that delineate clean geometric areas without interrupting the natural flow of content.

## Shapes

The shape system utilizes a curated radius range (10px–14px) to balance scholarly precision with organic softness.

### Radius Scale
- **Base (0.5rem / 8px - 10px)**: Input fields, buttons, dropdown items, table cells, and internal chips.
- **Large (`rounded-lg`, 0.75rem - 1rem / 12px - 14px)**: Standard application cards, administrative panels, document inspection containers, and dialog modals.
- **Extra-Large (`rounded-xl`, 1.25rem - 1.5rem / 20px - 24px)**: Outer app shells, banner cards, and prominent focal modules.
- **Full (`rounded-full`)**: Status pills, avatars, counter badges, and circular action icons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#6D9159`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius 10px. Hover transitions to `#5C7C4B`. Active press scales subtly (`0.98`). Font weight: 600 (`label-lg`).
- **Secondary / Institutional**: Background `#334A26`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius 10px. Used for formal administrative actions (e.g., "Approve Mobility Dossier"). Hover: `#273A1C`.
- **Tertiary / Subdued**: Background `#E9F1E4`, text `#334A26`, border `1px solid transparent`. Hover: border `#6D9159`, background `#E0ECD9`.
- **Ghost**: Background transparent, text `#334A26`. Hover: background `#E9F1E4`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs & Selects**: Height 44px, surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #D8E3D2`, text `#11160E`, radius 10px, padding `0 14px`. Focus ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(109, 145, 89, 0.25)` with border color shifting to `#6D9159`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Checked background `#6D9159` with crisp white checkmark/dot. Unchecked border `1.5px solid #334A26` on `#FFFFFF` surface. Radius: 4px for checkboxes, full circle for radios.
- **Toggle Switches**: Track uses `#E9F1E4` when inactive; vibrant `#6D9159` when active. Thumb is `#FFFFFF` with soft shadow, height/width 20px with smooth cubic-bezier transition.

### Cards & Data Panels
- **Work Cards**: `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #D8E3D2`, radius 12px to 14px, internal padding `space-lg`.
- **Summary & Metric Containers**: `#E9F1E4` surface, no outer shadow, subtle `1px solid rgba(51, 74, 38, 0.08)`, radius 12px. Headings in `#11160E`, contextual highlights in `#334A26`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills**: Height 24px, radius `9999px`, padding `2px 10px`.
  - *Submitted / Under Review*: Background `#E9F1E4`, text `#334A26`.
  - *Approved / Active*: Background `rgba(109, 145, 89, 0.15)`, text `#334A26`, indicator dot `#6D9159`.
  - *Urgent / Incomplete*: Background `rgba(176, 75, 53, 0.12)`, text `#8E2A18`.

### Specialized Mobility Management Components
- **Bilateral Agreement Timeline**: Vertical progress ribbon using a 2px `#D8E3D2` spine, with `#6D9159` completed nodes and `#334A26` active state rings enclosing academic milestones (Nomination, Learning Agreement, Visa Approval, Arrival Validation).
- **Academic Dossier Drawer**: Right-anchored sheet featuring `#FFFFFF` background, layered review tiers, and sticky action footers framed by `#E9F1E4` divider lines.
